# Animal Well

**Date Played:** April 2026 | **Rating:** 10.0/10 | **Genre:** Metroidvania, Puzzle | **System:** Retroid Pocket Flip 2 | **Hours Played:** 28 hours

This was truly a unique experience. The lonely, mysterious setting is mirrored perfectly in the gameplay. No NPC or popup tutorial will show you how to conquer obstacles, or direct you in your exploration. Each new tool feels like an open-ended puzzle. Late into the game I was still learning new ways to utilize tools I had received in it's opening hours. The feeling of unraveling these mysteries is like nothing I have felt before. It takes the mind blowing moments of a puzzle game and fuzes it with a joys of a precision platformer letting you take ownership for your successes in ways neither genre can accomplish on it's own. Rarely do I know an hour into a game that it will be one of my all time favorites. Rarer still does it end up surpassing these expectations.
