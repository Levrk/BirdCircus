# Returnal

**Date Played:** July 2026 | **Rating:** 9.5/10 | **Genre:** Roguelike, Action | **System:** PC | **Hours Played:** 46 hours

3D action platformer roguelike bullet hell. If you think that is too many genres to comfortably fit into one game you are wrong. The story is Alien meets Interstellar, but also I haven’t seen Alien so that might be wrong. You can tell from watching 2 seconds of gameplay that this game is something special. It is pure action with each moment of gameplay containing dozens of enemies vomiting unique arrays of colorful projectiles at Selene as she zips through lovecraftian worlds sliding in and out of combat with quick air dashes and laser sword slashes. 

The gunplay is superb, the enemies are well designed if not quite varied enough, and the story is told through a good blend of environmental winks and esoteric cutscenes. I had looked forward to playing this game for about 5 years since first watching a video essay by Redacted, and after finally getting a PC strong enough to play it I was not disappointed.
