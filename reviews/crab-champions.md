# Crab Champions

**Date Played:** December 2023 | **Rating:** 8.0/10 | **Genre:** Third-Person Shooter, Roguelike | **System:** Steam Deck | **Hours Played:** 53 hours

This is everything a rogue-like should be: fast, frantic, begging for one more run. It leans heavy on gameplay, and doesn't take itself too seriously. I love the fact that you are a crab, and the vibrant colors are easy on the eyes (even if the art just looks like unreal store assets). What really makes this thing shine is the movement. Chaining slides and jumps adds a layer of depth that keeps things engaging. The satisfying gameplay was enough to keep me chasing achievements for well over 50hrs. I think after about 65 hours I am all done on this one until it comes out of early access. Can't wait to reach victory island in 1.0.
