# Resident Evil 2 (Remake)

**Date Played:** March 2023 | **Rating:** 9.0/10 | **Genre:** Survival Horror, Third-Person Shooter | **System:** Steam Deck | **Hours Played:** 9 hours

First and only Resident Evil game I have played. While I don't think this series is really my thing in terms of gameplay, I loved my time with this one. MR. X is the scariest video game villain I have ever had the pleasure of running away from.
