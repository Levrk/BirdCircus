# Super Hot

**Date Played:** April 2024 | **Rating:** 8.0/10 | **Genre:** First-Person Shooter | **System:** Steam Deck | **Hours Played:** 4 hours

I have never seen a John Wick movie but I imagine they go something like this. Few games have made me feel as cool as this one. I also appreciated the short run time. It is an awesome concept, executed to perfection, and ending on a high note.
