# Fields of Mistria

**Date Played:** August 2026 | **Rating:** 8.5/10 | **Genre:** Farming Sim | **System:** PC | **Hours Played:** 49 hours

This is the perfect cozy game. The characters are so well written on multiple occasions I found myself running over to my girlfriend because I just needed her to see the charming thing somebody had just done or said. Each system is a near perfect balance of complexity, although I do think this balance is what ultimately makes the game grow stale at a certain point. No one system is deep enough to fully grab my attention, so I ended up bouncing between activities happily until I felt like I had gotten what I needed from the game and moved on. I won’t hold that against Fields of Mistria. I think even a farming sim is meant to be set aside eventually, and this one certainly did enough to stand out. Between the lovable characters and rpg(lite) upgrade system and spells, this deserves to be recognized as more than just a stardew clone.
