# Final Fantasy Tactics

**Date Played:** September 2026 | **Rating:** 9.5/10 | **Genre:** Tactical RPG, JRPG | **System:** Retroid Pocket Flip 2, RG SP | **Hours Played:** 50 hours

Damn I can’t believe this game was made almost 30 years ago. Despite missing the fully animated cutscenes of the recent remake, the original holds up exceptionally well. The class system made character upgrades rewarding (up to a certain point), and I was shocked to find a plot as complex and intriguing as any I have found in gaming. It was actually my first real foray into this genre and left me eager to try out some similar titles. While the difficulty was crushing at times (I did occasionally abuse save state), scraping my way through that final fight was incredible. I will never forget the sacrifices made by MungoTina and QuailForce, nor forgive Lord Orlandu for going berzerk and trying to kill me at the last second.
