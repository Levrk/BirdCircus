# Risk of Rain 2

**Date Played:** November 2022 | **Rating:** 7.5/10 | **Genre:** Third-Person Shooter, Roguelike | **System:** Nintendo Switch | **Hours Played:** 10 hours

I get why people love this game, but it didn’t quite click with me. I usually favor roguelikes with some level of meta progression and no class system, this is not that. I also didn’t love the gunplay or the graphics, which is kinda damning. I also like fully exploring every corner of a level to maximize my power before moving forward, which contrasts with ROR2’s unique difficulty scaling system. At every step of the way this game’s design clashed with the way I wanted to play it. Does this mean I am bad? Does this mean the game is bad? I would say no to both.
