# The Incident at Galley House

**Date Played:** August 2026 | **Rating:** 8.5/10 | **Genre:** Puzzle | **System:** Mac | **Hours Played:** 12 hours

This game is a spooky who-done-it gone supernatural with incredible pacing. It has compelling characters and a mechanical hook that helps it stand out despite an arguably forgettable visual style. I found myself getting exponentially more invested over the course of my roughly 10 hour playthrough, and playing it alongside my girlfriend solidified this as an awesome experience.
