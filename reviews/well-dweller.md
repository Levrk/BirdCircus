# Well Dweller

**Date Played:** October 2026 | **Rating:** 9.0/10 | **Genre:** Metroidvania | **System:** PC, Retroid Pocket Flip 2 | **Hours Played:** 14 hours

It is hard to review this game without talking about hollowknight. Well Dweller does enough to stand out, but not enough to fully escape the obvious comparisons. while it may fall short of team cherry's atmosphere and gruling combat, It distinguishes itself through deep puzzle platforming and a cast of adorable creatures. 

I was drawn to this game after hearing it described by Giant Bomb as "silksong but nice" and after 100 percenting this game in about 13 hours I think the comparison rings true. I think some of this kindness is great, but it does at times go to far in encouraging the player to engage with systems which trivialize combat and exploration. I really wish trinket slots weren't so easily found. The ability to equip most of my trinkets at once ruined any sense of buildcrafting. The bosses were so beautifully grotesque but most of them only took me 1 or 2 attempts. 

I think this game would have been a 10/10 with a few more balancing adjustements, but I loved it regardless. The menagerie of platforming mechanics left me feeling like my solution to any given puzzle really was my own. They were also intuitive enough that I felt confident running full speed through an array of spikes knowing I could likely swing, bounce, or float my way to safety by the seat of my pants.
