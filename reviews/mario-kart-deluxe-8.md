# Mario Kart Deluxe 8

**Date Played:** April 2022 | **Rating:** 8.5/10 | **Genre:** Racing | **System:** Nintendo Switch | **Hours Played:** 30 hours

Mario Kart is a great multiplayer game for non gamers. It is easy to control, and the rubber banding mechanics mean skill level won’t hold you back from having a good time. Outside of the simple grand-prixs the game is lacking in content, additional maps are locked behind DLC and additional game modes lack much thought. Regardless, the game is something special. The courses are some of the best the series has ever had, and the driving mechanics are simple but rewarding. Mario Kart is quantity over quality and style over substances, it does what it does exceptionally well and that is enough.
