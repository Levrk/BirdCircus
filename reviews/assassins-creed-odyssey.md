# Assassin's Creed Odyssey

**Date Played:** March 2020 | **Rating:** 8.5/10 | **Genre:** Open World, RPG | **System:** Xbox | **Hours Played:** 242 hours

This was my pandemic game, and turned out to be the perfect world to be set loose in at a time when I was stuck inside. The plot was mediocre and the combat was repetitive, but galavanting through mediterranean came to define my memories of early lock down. The open sea and vast number of islands to explore were compelling enough that I completed 100% of it, even minor objectives. It did not hurt that this was my first modern open world game, so simply moving from checkpoint to checkpoint didn't get stale even after well over 200 hours. The ancient greek lore certainly sweetened the deal, as like many others of my generation I grew up obsessed with Percy Jackson. My views on this game have certainly adjusted as I explore more of what gaming has to offer, but I will always have a soft spot for Oddysey.
