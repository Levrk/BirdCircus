# Skyrim

**Date Played:** November 2020 | **Rating:** 10.0/10 | **Genre:** Open World, RPG | **System:** Xbox, Steam Deck | **Hours Played:** 104 hours

The brilliance of Skyrim is difficult to overstate. Few have spent time in its vast enchanting world and not found a piece of themselves stuck therein. While I may have first played it on my own in 2020, it was a world I was familiar with from time spent passing a controller back and forth at a friend’s house in 2012. I always knew of it as this mind blowing digitization of a feeling I had only gotten from dungeons and dragons. 

The world of Skyrim feels alive in a way that no other game has replicated even 15 years later. The sheer quantity of interesting content makes this game as dense as a black hole, without ever feeling like quality over quantity. After over a hundred hours I still feel like a newcomer in Tamriel. I have had some issues with getting mods setup otherwise I would have played much more of this game.
